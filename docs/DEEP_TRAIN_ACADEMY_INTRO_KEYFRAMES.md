# Deep Train Academy — 10 hyper-real keyframes (video base plates)

**Viewer takeaway in 10 seconds:** *I am a human teaching a naive AI body to get smarter — my way.*

**Look lock:** RAW live-action photograph. Arri Alexa / 35mm anamorphic.  
**Never:** cartoon, anime, illustration, painterly, plastic game render, stylized 3D.

**Human:** Same person all 10 shots — 30s explorer, hiking gear, headlamp, real skin pores/sweat.  
**AI companion:** Same physical android/cyborg — synthetic skin, subtle teal seam lines, LED chest/ temple indicators that **brighten as it learns** (dim → confident).

## Per-shot prompts

### intro_01 — Human alone
Real human explorer in wet Florida karst lava tube, headlamp beam, teal distant work lights, determined expression, no robot visible, documentary cave photography.

### intro_02 — Dormant AI found
Same human finds slumped humanoid android against cave wall, powered off, blank faceplate, dim chest light off, human's hand reaching toward it, emotional discovery.

### intro_03 — First wake
Same human kneeling as android powers on, confused naive empty eyes opening, faint teal chest glow booting, intimate two-shot, shallow DOF.

### intro_04 — Human demonstrates
Same human stepping across natural stone path over cave void, android standing aside watching with head tilted like a student, human in motion demonstrating.

### intro_05 — AI fails
Same android mid-stumble on stones, awkward wrong footing, same human catching its arm, frustration and patience on human face, comedy-tragedy realism not cartoon.

### intro_06 — Human teaches
Same human both hands on android shoulders correcting posture, eye contact, teacher and student, android hunched naive, dim teal glow, intimate coaching moment.

### intro_07 — Checkpoint together
Same pair at metal gate in cave, human pointing forward, android stepping toward gate, teamwork beginning, teal LED gate strip.

### intro_08 — AI improving
Same android successfully crossing ledge, human in background nodding, android chest glow brighter, visible progress, photoreal motion.

### intro_09 — Training mimicry
Same human gesturing path direction, android mirroring arm movement, student copying teacher, karst tunnel depth, partnership forming.

### intro_10 — Growth finale
Same human and android running or striding side by side equal pace through beautiful cave, matched confident movement, android glow steady bright, human slight smile, trust and mastery.
